How to use this program

  1. Browse Menu; search by name or select Drinks or Food.
  2. Choose a whole-number quantity up to 10 and available stock, then Add to Cart.
  3. Open Your Order to update or remove items.
  4. Select fulfillment, add instructions, optionally enter CAFE10 and a budget, and save settings.
  5. Review the total and place the order. Save the receipt number.
  6. Open Sales Report to filter orders by date/category and compare item sales.

Local Business Digital Ordering System

This student project lets cafe customers browse products, manage a cart, calculate costs, stay within a budget, and review saved orders. Inventory checks prevent selling unavailable products. Sales summaries help a local business understand demand.
