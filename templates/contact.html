How much does delivery cost?

Pickup is free. Simulated delivery adds $3.99. No real deliveries are dispatched.

Can I use a discount?

Enter CAFE10 in Your Order for 10% off the items subtotal, before tax. Delivery is not discounted.

Where can I find an old receipt?

Open Sales report & history and select an order number. A receipt can be refreshed without placing another order.

From menu to receipt

  1. Find your favorites.

    Search the menu or browse Drinks and Food.

  2. Choose your quantities.

    Add up to 10 of each item, within available stock.

  3. Make it your order.

    Update items, choose pickup or simulated delivery, and add any instructions.

  4. Review the total.

    Add an optional discount or budget, then select Update total.

  5. Place your demo order.

    Keep the saved receipt or find it in the order history.

About this demonstration

Rendezvous Cafe is a student project set in Cumming, Georgia. A verified pickup address and real contact details have not been supplied. No real orders are sent. For allergies or dietary needs, contact a real café directly.
