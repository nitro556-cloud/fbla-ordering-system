The idea behind Rendezvous

A place to meet.
A simple way
to order.

Rendezvous begins with a familiar moment: choosing a coffee and something to eat.

Built for FBLA Introduction to Programming, this student project explores how a local café can make ordering clearer—from browsing the menu to understanding the final total.

The café concept is set in Cumming, Georgia. Every order here is a demonstration: no payments are accepted and no deliveries are dispatched.

Take a look at the menu ↗

Considered from
menu to receipt.

Live inventory, clear prices, an optional spending limit, and a saved receipt make it easier to understand every choice. Sales reporting gives the café a view of orders and demand.
