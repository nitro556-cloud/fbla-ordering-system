Our Story

Rendezvous Cafe is a student-built local business ordering demonstration for FBLA Introduction to Programming. Our goal is to make browsing food, managing quantities, and understanding purchase totals easy.

The cafe concept is set in Cumming, Georgia. This prototype does not accept payments or dispatch deliveries.
